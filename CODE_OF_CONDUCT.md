# Community code of conduct

We are building a serious review culture across different artistic traditions and levels of experience. Be specific about the work, generous toward people, transparent about uncertainty, and open to correction.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, plagiarism accusations presented without due process, identity inference from style, non-consensual publication, review brigading, and attempts to purchase benchmark outcomes.

Moderators may edit, hide, lock, or remove material and restrict participation to protect creators and the community. Report sensitive concerns privately when possible.
